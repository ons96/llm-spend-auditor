How the cost is computed

Monthly cost = input millions x price per million input tokens + output millions x price per million output tokens. Nothing is cached, batched, or discounted on your behalf.

Why the base price is a median

The current model is priced at the median across the providers in this snapshot, not the cheapest listing. The cheapest listing is often a stale reseller discount and would understate your bill.

Quality is never assumed

A model without an Artificial Analysis score is never recommended and never compared. Every saving shown sits next to the index points it costs, because "95% cheaper" says nothing about what you lose.
